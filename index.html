06 / LAZY REALISM FX

Before you get started.

A few practical details.

Adjust for your scene

Effects may need tuning for your scene scale, selected geometry and intended look. Start with one effect, then build up the result.

Plan your preview

Particle counts, instances and detailed geometry affect performance. Use a smaller setup while adjusting your shot, then increase detail as needed.

Can I combine different effects?

Yes. Build up your environment with effects that complement one another, such as wind, leaves and dust, or rain, droplets and puddles.

Does every effect work the same way?

No. Some tools add geometry or motion, while others change surface shading. Follow the workflow for the effect you are using.

Where do I check Blender compatibility?

Check the supported Blender versions and the download notes on this product listing before installing.

Where should I start?

Choose one effect, add it to a simple test scene and adjust its controls. Use the product documentation and tutorials for the full workflow.
