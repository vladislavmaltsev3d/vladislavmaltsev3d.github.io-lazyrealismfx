Limitations.

  • Physical Leaves is an artist-directed procedural interpretation, not a one-to-one reproduction of real-world physics. Its landing behavior uses custom algorithms: for example, leaves may settle smoothly into place instead of bouncing or reacting with perfectly photorealistic physical accuracy. This approach is intentional and prioritizes simple setup, predictable results, lighter calculations and faster performance.
  • Tree Wind has been tested on several types of trees and is designed to work well out of the box. Perfect behavior on every tree species, mesh structure and topology cannot be guaranteed. Some individual trees may require manual adjustment of wind, bending or mask values to achieve the best result.
  • Very high-poly trees may reduce performance. Trees containing approximately 200,000 polygons or more can produce slower viewport playback and processing, depending on the hardware, scene complexity and selected settings.
  • Shader compatibility varies between Eevee and Cycles. Tire Marks, Footprints, Fingerprints, Scratches, Simple Rust, Dirt Particles, Ground Dirt, Edge Wear, Snow and Puddles work in both engines. Leaks, Pro Moss and Pro Dust have limited functionality in Eevee. Oil Stains, Dirt in Edges and Moss Leaks require Cycles. Pro Rust and Pro Dirt work fully in Cycles; in Eevee, their edge masks are unavailable while the Wall and Ground sections remain functional. Cycles is recommended for the complete Pro Shader experience.
